# OFFGRID / creative system

Industrial editorial. Bone #F2F0E9, void #101010, signal #E34A35, dust #C5C0B5 and soft rules #D8D4CB. Darkened neutral utility text is used on bone to improve contrast. Archivo Black display; Space Grotesk body and controls; IBM Plex Mono identifiers. Hard edges, generous gutters, native document scroll, asymmetric product and lookbook compositions. No generic commerce shell, fake cart or checkout.

The complete-page generated concept established rhythm; a separate desktop hero concept clarified first-viewport hierarchy. Generated concepts are design references, never flattened UI. Required brief copy takes precedence over invented concept-image text. In particular, no generated search/cart controls, claims of water repellency, social accounts or previous real collections were implemented.

Composition: compact black announcement, bone sticky header, full-height photo with left-aligned oversized title, editorial manifesto, dark split drop introduction, staggered three-column product grid, giant typographic statement, dark asymmetric six-image lookbook, macro garment detail with ruled principles, two offset archive studies, quiet newsletter, wide closing campaign and transparent concept footer. Mobile becomes a vertical editorial sequence with a two-column collection (one column below 360px) and native dialog navigation.

Motion: short GSAP hero timeline, restrained once-only ScrollTrigger headings, filter transitions, menu link stagger. All motion is conditional on no reduced-motion preference. No scroll hijacking or loading screen.
